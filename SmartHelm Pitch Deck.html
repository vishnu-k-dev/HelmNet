09 · Drowsiness Detection

We use two clinical-grade triggers — not guesswork.

Trigger 1
Continuous Eye Closure
What it measures
Eyes closed for longer than 1.5 seconds without interruption.
→ Immediate alert fires
Catches: microsleeps, sudden nodding, head-drop events
Trigger 2
PERCLOS
What it measures
Percentage of time eyes are closed over any 60-second rolling window.
→ Alert when ≥ 30%
Catches: cumulative fatigue, gradual drowsiness buildup, slow blink drift

Both triggers cross-validate in real time. One false reading does not fire an alert.

Detection Engine
  • MediaPipe FaceLandmarker468-point landmark tracking per frame

Processing Layer
  • Python + OpenCVReal-time EAR computation
